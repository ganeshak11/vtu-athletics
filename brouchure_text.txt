PAGE 5 – About Dr. ACS Aura – 2026
Title: About the Athletic Meet
Suggested content:
Dr. ACS Aura – 2026, the VTU 28th State Level Inter-Collegiate Athletic Meet, is a prestigious sporting event bringing together student athletes from engineering institutions affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi.
Scheduled from 7th to 10th December 2026 at Dr. ACS College of Engineering, Bengaluru, the event aims to provide a competitive platform for young athletes to showcase their talent, endurance, speed, strength and sporting excellence.
The four-day athletic meet will celebrate the true spirit of sportsmanship while encouraging healthy competition, mutual respect, teamwork and the pursuit of excellence.
Objectives of the Meet
To promote sports culture and physical fitness among engineering students.
To identify and encourage emerging athletic talent.
To provide a competitive platform for university-level athletes.
To foster unity and interaction among VTU-affiliated institutions.
To inculcate discipline, leadership and team spirit.
To encourage participation, inclusivity and excellence in sports.
Event highlights:
Four days of athletic competitions.
Participation from VTU-affiliated engineering colleges.
Track and field competitions.
Recognition of outstanding athletic performances.
Medals, certificates and trophies for winners.
Celebration of sporting excellence and camaraderie.

PAGE 8 – Rules and Regulations
Title: General Rules and Regulations
This page is essential for team managers, coaches and participating athletes.
Include the following sections:
1. Eligibility
Only eligible students from VTU-affiliated institutions may participate, subject to university regulations.
Participants must produce valid institutional identity cards.
Eligibility shall be verified by the organising committee.

2. Registration
Participating colleges must submit entries within the stipulated deadline.
Final entries must be approved by the concerned authorities.
Team managers shall be responsible for the correctness of participant details.
3. Reporting
Athletes must report to the event venue well before the scheduled competition.
Late reporting may result in disqualification as per competition rules.
Bib numbers must be displayed appropriately.
4. Competition Rules
Events will be conducted in accordance with the applicable VTU and athletics competition regulations.
Decisions of the technical officials and jury shall be final, subject to prescribed appeal procedures.
Athletes must follow the instructions of the organising committee.
5. Discipline and Sportsmanship
All participants must maintain discipline and decorum.
Misconduct or unsportsmanlike behaviour may attract disciplinary action.
Athletes must respect officials, opponents and fellow participants.
6. Safety
Appropriate sportswear and footwear are mandatory.
Participants must follow safety instructions.
Any injury or medical emergency must be immediately reported.
The final rulebook should be based on the official VTU circular and applicable Athletics Federation of India regulations.
